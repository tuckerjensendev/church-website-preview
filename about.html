From The Admin Panel

The page can stay orderly while the story becomes fully their own.

  • Replace the mission statement and church history in plain language
  • Swap welcome photos, portraits, or ministry images as needed
  • Keep the layout consistent even when several people help update it

A Gentle First Read

This is where visitors begin to understand the heart of a church.

A church can use this page to share who they are, what shapes their ministry, and what kind of community someone will meet when they walk through the doors.

Mission

Share the reason the church exists.

A short mission statement, a few grounding convictions, and one warm paragraph often do more than pages of explanation. All of that can be rewritten whenever needed.

Leadership

Introduce pastors and key leaders with warmth.

Leadership names, short bios, or pastoral welcome copy can be updated without changing the page structure or disturbing the rest of the site.

History

Explain how the Lord has shaped the church.

If a church wants to add its history, neighborhood story, or vision for the future, the page is ready for that without becoming cluttered.

Consistent Layouts

New pages can feel different without becoming disorganized.

When a church adds a new page, it can choose from a few page arrangements that already fit the site well. That keeps the whole website steady and familiar from page to page.

  • Choose a layout that suits the content instead of starting from scratch
  • Keep text, photos, and buttons easy to edit within a clear structure
  • Use the same approach for ministries, events, or special seasonal pages
Rearrange the welcome A church can rewrite the opening message and keep the page centered on its own story.
Refresh the visuals Photos and logos can change over time without breaking the design.

Keep It True

Every church should be able to make the words sound like their own voice.

This layout is only the starting point. A church can turn it into its real mission, real story, and real welcome without needing to open the code.
