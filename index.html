Free Church Website Preview

A welcoming church website your team can truly manage.

This preview shows a calm, mobile-friendly church website template that can be handed to a church as a blank slate. Staff can update the words, photos, colors, buttons, footer details, and navigation from the admin panel without touching code.

What Staff Can Adjust

Everything stays organized, but nothing feels locked down.

Churches can make the site feel like their own in one place, while the page structure stays clean and consistent for visitors.

  • Swap in a real church logo, photos, and welcome message
  • Change colors and accents for a warmer or brighter feel
  • Rename or hide buttons like Visit, Watch, Give, or Contact
  • Add new pages and place navigation links on the left or right
  • Choose which page opens first at the main web address
  • Preview changes before they are finalized

Made For Ministry

Built to feel simple for church staff, not just for web people.

The goal is not to give churches a pile of settings. The goal is to give them an admin panel that feels calm, clear, and forgiving, so a pastor, assistant, or office volunteer can keep the website current without confusion.

Refresh the whole look in one sitting.

A Site That Can Breathe

When ministry changes, the website can change with it.

Service times, footer details, call-to-action buttons, and the first page shown at the main domain can all be adjusted from the admin panel. That means the site can respond quickly to Easter, Christmas, a new sermon series, or a shift in weekly rhythms.

  • Keep Sunday details current without waiting on a developer
  • Move navigation links as the site grows over time
  • Replace paragraphs one at a time without disturbing the layout
One place for key details Service times, address, phone number, and footer links stay easy to find and easy to update.
Three page styles New pages can stay consistent while still giving variety in how content is arranged.
Preview before confirming Staff can check how a page will look before settling on the change.
