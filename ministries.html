Highlight ministries in a way that feels clear at a glance.

Flexible, But Ordered

Each section stays easy to understand for both staff and visitors.

  • Add a photo, a short paragraph, and a few highlights for each ministry
  • Turn the section button on or off depending on what the church needs
  • Keep new pages visually consistent without starting over each time

Ministry Paths

Group ministries so people can see where they belong.

Whether a church wants to describe children’s ministry, small groups, outreach, classes, or care ministries, this layout keeps the information tidy and easy to scan.

01

Children & Families

Show where young families can connect with confidence.

Nursery details, children’s check-in, youth gatherings, and volunteer information can all be updated as a church grows or a schedule changes.

  • Rewrite the section title and body at any time
  • Swap in a new photo when the ministry changes
  • Keep one simple button if a next step is helpful
02

Adults & Discipleship

Explain classes, studies, and small groups without overcrowding the page.

Churches can use the same format for Bible studies, membership classes, recovery groups, or seasonal classes while still keeping the page light and readable.

  • Add key highlights in bullet form when details matter
  • Turn section buttons on or off depending on the season
  • Keep the same visual rhythm across multiple ministries
03

Outreach & Service

Point people toward opportunities to serve and bless the community.

If a church begins a new outreach effort, mission emphasis, or benevolence ministry, a new page or section can be added without breaking the overall site structure.

  • Add a new ministry page as new needs arise
  • Place its navigation link where it fits best in the header
  • Preview the page before it becomes part of the public site

Room To Grow

As ministries change, the website can stay current without becoming messy.

A church can start with a simple ministries page and gradually add more pages over time. Navigation links can be renamed, rearranged, or removed so the site never feels frozen.

  • Add a new page for a school, outreach, or seasonal event
  • Move a link to the left or right side of the header
  • Keep the footer links in step with the main navigation
Change the arrangement Navigation can move as the site grows, while the centered logo stays anchored.
